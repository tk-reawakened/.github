# RPG Toolkit Reawakened

**Your game, completely and freely.**

A tribute to RPG Toolkit, and an effort to bring its games and its community back.

In the early 2000s, Christopher B. Matthews' RPG Toolkit opened a door for a whole generation: a free tool made for role-playing games, and a community where young people made their first maps, wrote their first scripts and shared their first games. Since then the sites have gone, the games have scattered, and the tool only runs on Windows machines of twenty years ago.

RPG Toolkit Reawakened is not RPG Toolkit, and it doesn't try to replace it. It's a new project, built from scratch for the web, with one hope: that the games keep running and the people have somewhere to gather again.

## The projects

**[TK Reawakened](https://github.com/tk-reawakened/rpgtoolkit-reawakened)** is the engine and the editor. Open a classic RPG Toolkit game from a link and play it the way it used to; draw maps, design characters and script battles in an editor that runs in your browser; send your game to a friend as a link. It's open source under the MIT license, so what you make is yours to share, change or sell. It's its own project with its own version line, and its first release will be v1.0.

**[Ludothèque](https://github.com/tk-reawakened/ludotheque)** is the recovered game corpus: historical RPG Toolkit 2/3 games preserved, with the original archived page text and packaging, as reference fixtures for compatibility testing and preservation research.

## Where it stands

It's early days, and nothing is playable yet. The recovery work is under way, and nothing is published until the people involved have had their say. The full written specification and architecture are done, and we're setting up the project's workspace and checks. The first goal is the player: a classic community game playing start to finish in a browser. Our first candidate is *Final Destiny*, by Christopher B. Matthews himself. The editor comes right after.

## Helping out

We'd love the help. The earlier revivals stalled when too few people were left carrying them, so this one is meant to be built together. Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/tk-reawakened/rpgtoolkit-reawakened/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), which has the commands to build and test the project and the few rules that keep the project's license clean.
